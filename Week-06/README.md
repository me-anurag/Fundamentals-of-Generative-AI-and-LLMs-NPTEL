# Week 06: Advanced GAN Variants and Applications

Part of the Fundamentals of Generative AI and Large Language Models: Theory and Practice - NPTEL repository.

## Topics Covered

- Conditional GAN (cGAN)
- CycleGAN (image-to-image translation)
- Pix2Pix
- StyleGAN and StyleGAN2
- GAN evaluation metrics: Inception Score (IS), FID
- Real-world applications: art generation, super-resolution, medical imaging augmentation

## Hands-On Exercise

Train a Conditional GAN for class-controlled image generation. Apply a pre-trained CycleGAN for style transfer (horses to zebras, or Monet painting style). Compute the FID score between generated and real samples.
